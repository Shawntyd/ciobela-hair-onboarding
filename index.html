The setup

What you're stepping into

  • You operate as an independent 1099 hair stylist — your brand, your schedule, your clients, your income.

Your business

Your clients stay yours

  • You're an established professional, and your book is yours. Bring your clientele with you.
  • Your own pricing, your own schedule, your own services. We provide the space; you run the business.
  • We build your public profile and booking presence so your clients can find you and rebook easily.
The advantage

Why Cio Bela

  • A finished, high-end environment. Your clients walk into a space that matches the level of your work.
  • Real support. Cio Bela handles the building, the amenities, and the brand presence so you can focus on your chair.
  • A full-service ecosystem. Hair, nails, lashes, waxing, and tanning under one roof means more reasons for clients to visit and to stay.
